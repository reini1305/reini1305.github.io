I've recently acquired a Palm IIIxe in near mint condition with the intention to replace my Remarkable Move for daily journaling.

{{< img
  src="palm.jpg"
  alt="Cat"
  caption="Palm IIIxe running may journaling app." >}}

My daily ritual is as follows:

- Check mails and messages over night from away teams
- Write down to-dos for the day
- Write down meetings I plan to join
- Keep track of start and end of the workday

and then ticking stuff off as I get to it. To help me do it I made this [small app](https://github.com/reini1305/palm-worklog) which I'm ready to share now after a month of fiddling. It's very much tailored to my needs, but maybe someone may find it useful as well. In addition to the points above, you can also sync logbooks between Palms via IR (got a cheap Tungsten E as second machine). It has keyboard shortcuts for the most needed entries to support the folding keyboard I got for myself.
